## About me

I'm a Computer Science developer who enjoys working across **web applications, AI, automation, games, and business software**. Sometimes a project solves a real problem; sometimes I build it just to find out whether an idea can work.

I enjoy both the technical and human sides of development: understanding workflows, working with people, and making things that are pleasant to use. I've also worked around manufacturing environments, where practical software matters more than fancy buzzwords.

Outside of code, I'm into **anime, games, and a good story**. I like cozy games, thoughtful worldbuilding, and romance that actually makes you care about the characters.
